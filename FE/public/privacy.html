Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

VdoGen ("we", "us", or "our") operates vdogen.prasadev.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

By using VdoGen, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated by your use of the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you create an account.
  • Prompts and content — the text prompts you submit to generate animations.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, generation counts, and session duration.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our payment provider. We do not store card details.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, and referring URL.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the VdoGen service.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To send transactional emails such as receipts and account notifications.
  • To improve the quality of AI-generated animations.
  • To monitor usage and enforce plan limits.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate VdoGen:

  • Clerk — authentication and user account management.
  • Stripe — payment processing for Prime subscriptions.
  • Cloudflare — CDN, edge serving, and infrastructure.

Each provider has their own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Data Retention

We retain your account information and generated videos for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Anonymised usage statistics may be retained indefinitely for product improvement purposes.

Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@vdogen.prasadev.com.
